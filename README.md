# Multimodal AI for Mechanical Property Prediction

Multimodal deep learning pipeline combining microscopic imaging and tabular data to predict the mechanical properties of cementitious materials — M2 internship project, L2MGC Laboratory (CY Cergy Paris Université).

## Overview

During my Master's internship in Applied Mathematics at CY Cergy Paris Université (L2MGC laboratory, 2026), I worked on the design of a **multimodal artificial intelligence pipeline** for predicting the mechanical properties of cementitious materials, combining three heterogeneous data sources:

- Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) images
- Experimental measurements
- Chemical descriptors

## Main Contributions

- Design of a **multimodal AI model** (vision + tabular data fusion) predicting mechanical properties from three heterogeneous data sources.
- Development of an **end-to-end data pipeline**: database design, data cleaning, model training, cross-validation, and reporting of results to the research team.
- Experimental evaluation of several machine learning and deep learning approaches for multimodal fusion.
